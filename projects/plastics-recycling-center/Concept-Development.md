## How does the shredder work?

The shredder is simple to use but mistakes can still be made. Put plastic into the funnel at the top while the machine is on, the teeth will crush/cut/rip at the plastic
making it smaller and smaller. Once small enough it will fall through a curved metal plate with holes that acts like a filter, after it falls through it will land in a
cart to be transported to another part of the recycling process.

## Collection Process

### Areas of collection

- PAC.
- Cafeteria.
- Phase 2.
- Offices.

### Transport

- Some sort of cart, either made of wood or recycled plastic, with wheels.
- Set up routes and adjust them accordingly.

### Sorting Process

- Take off paper and caps.
- Sort types of plastics.
- Put plastics into separate bins.

### Storage

- Have bins, either wood or plastic; to hold plastic.

## Safety

### Personal

- Close toed shoes.
- Gloves and eye protection when near machines.
- Masks for inhilation protection.

### Machine + Equipment

- Stay alert.
- Follow warnings.
- Keep hands/arms/legs/hair from hot or crushing things.
- Keep areas trip free and clean.
- Supervisors

### Storage

- Crates can only be stacked 3 high.
- Crates must be placed in their zones/area.
- Crates cannot be sticking out or in a walkway.
