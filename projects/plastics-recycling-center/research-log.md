### Research Findings

#### Finding 1: [Plastics cause the death and destruction of wild life, the environment, and us humans.]

**What I found:**  
Plastics cause many issues as they poison the creatures around them. Single use trash and old thrown away tools/supplies can trap, harm, and even kill wild life. Humans are not safe from this,
microplastics poison our bloodstreams and thrown away metals can cut, trap, and poison our children.

**Why it matters to our project:**  
The economy and social worlds will collapse as tourism, fishing, and hunting become soon extinct. Fishing is taking heavy losses as trash kill the fish, break our equipment and boats, even poisoning
the fish we do catch making it unsafe to eat. Tourism in places with trash issues are near zero as people don't wish to see or possibly get hurt by such trash. Hunting is becoming difficult as forests
are being destroyed for dumps, burned for fuel, the climates becoming harsh and unlivable, and the wild life chased away. These issues effect how us, the people, live our lives, addressing plastic waste
allows people to become aware of these problems and see how this will only end with death, driving them to help.

**Source:**  
[PLASTIC POLLUTION: A GLOBAL PROBLEM THAT STARTS WITH US](https://smea.uw.edu/currents/plastic-pollution-a-global-problem-that-starts-with-us/)

#### Finding 2: [Is recycling plastic beneficial and does it actually help?]

**What I found:**  
Recycling is one of the many ways to slow down and clean up the plastics and trash in the environment. A few other processes are, restricting single-use products, make laws on trash dumping, promoting
reuse/refillable systems, innovation, and even more. Recycling needs to also be a more accessible and affordable practice.

**Why it matters to our project:**  
Without recycling and it's other practices trash would be everywhere, piled high. Some people can't recycle as they don't have a car to drive to recycling centers or don't have trash bins that are for recycling.

**Source:**  
[The Growing Challenge of Plastic Waste: Causes, Consequences, and Solutions](https://www.aiu.edu/blog/the-growing-challenge-of-plastic-waste-causes-consequences-and-solutions/)

#### Finding 3: [What plastics might our own schools supply and what is being done about it?]

**What I found:**  
Many schools use single-use plastics like food containers, bottles, and plastic utensils. Schools combat this by adding water fountains for less use of bottled water, more paper containers, recycling bins in the cafeteria and classrooms.  

**Why it matters to our project:**  
Schools are who teach the next generations, making them more aware of the issues and problems of pollutions can have positive impacts as they turn up more willing to recycle and help.

**Source:**  
[HOW STUDENTS CAN HELP REDUCE PLASTIC WASTE AT HOME AND SCHOOL](https://www.jaypeeschools-jpsnoida.edu.in/blogs/how-students-can-help-reduce-plastic-waste-at-home-and-school)
